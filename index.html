Features & Benefits

01

Hygienic & Reliable

Nonporous surface means no place for bacteria and mold to take root. When it looks clean, it is actually clean. Perfect for kitchens, bathrooms, and healthcare environments.

  • NSF/ANSI 51 certified for food contact
  • Resistant to bacteria growth
  • Easy to clean and maintain
02

Seamless Beauty

Create virtually seamless installations with inconspicuous seams. The material can be shaped and formed into unique designs that flow continuously across your space.

  • Invisible seam integration
  • Thermoformable for curves
  • Custom fabrication options
03

Renewable Surface

Minor scratches and stains can be easily removed with a light abrasive cleanser. The solid nature of Corian® means color runs all the way through the material.

  • Repairable and renewable
  • Maintains appearance over time
  • Long-lasting investment
04

Versatile Applications

From countertops to wall cladding, from furniture to lighting - Corian® adapts to any design challenge. Its versatility is limited only by your imagination.

  • Kitchen & bathroom surfaces
  • Commercial installations
  • Decorative elements & furniture
